## Our screens against the current ones (the goal): your direction 4, the current screens 0, same 0

- **Judge 1** (A was your direction) picked your direction. The two share content and layout almost exactly, so this comes down to visual language. A uses colour to carry meaning. Running agent cards get a warm tint while queued ones get a dashed outline, so you can tell them apart at a glance. Section bands are tinted by state (your move, running, done, Jira error).
