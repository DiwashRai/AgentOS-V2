## The twins against Linear (secondary): your direction 0, Linear 4, same 0

- **Judge 1** (A was your direction) picked Linear. B uses a neutral near-black palette with a clear grey scale for text: primary, then muted metadata, then faint IDs. A tints every surface blue-navy and sets activity text in heavy bold everywhere. A's chips use saturated borders and fills that shout louder than the issue titles (screens 2, 5 and 7). B's controls are also built more carefully. Its popovers are split into hairline-divided sections, and Grouping, Ordering and Completed get real select boxes with a sort-direction glyph (screens 4 and 6). A floats bare "Priority ⌄" text beside a lone caret button, with dead gaps and no dividers. B also gives each control its own icon: filter funnel, sliders, sort arrows, git-PR. A reuses the same hamburger glyph for filter, display, grouping and views, and gives Ordering a refresh icon.

Then give each control its own icon (funnel for Filter, sliders for Display, sort arrows for Ordering) in place of the reused hamburger glyph.
