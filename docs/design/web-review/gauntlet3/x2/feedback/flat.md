## The twins against Linear (secondary): your direction 0, Linear 4, same 0

- **Judge 1** (A was your direction) picked Linear. B builds its surfaces with hairline borders and dividers: popovers are split into sections, dropdowns look like fields, and the chat and diff panels are framed cards. A uses flat grey fills that blend into each other, so every screen reads soft and less structured. B's icons are specific and varied (sort arrows, sliders for display, a filter funnel, PR glyphs, a ringed status for In Review). A reuses one hamburger icon for Filter, Display, Grouping and List, and uses a green checkmark for both "In Review" and "Done". A's type scale is also off: "Engineering" and "Triage" are huge display headings that swamp the toolbars, and A leaves dead vertical gaps in the Display popover and the agent panel, while B keeps its titles at toolbar scale and its rhythm tight.
  - your direction's gap: Give A's surfaces structure. Put a 1px low-contrast border on cards, chips, popovers and the chat/diff panels, split popover sections with hairline dividers, and draw selects as bordered fields instead of bare text with a chevron. Also replace the generic hamburger glyph with specific icons: a funnel for Filter, sliders for Display, sort arrows for Ordering.
  - Linear's flaw: Fix B's issue-detail layout so the agent chat panel no longer covers the right edge of the activity comment cards. Either make the activity column narrower to end before the panel or dock the panel in its own column. Also lift the very low-contrast secondary text (activity meta lines, IDs) at least one step brighter.
  - Broken: B screen 1: the floating agent panel overlaps the activity comment and "connected" cards, which are cut off at their right edge. B screen 2: the "Recent" tab and the "Currency"/"Frontend" label pills are clipped at the right viewport edge. A screen 2: the label pills are truncated to "Currency S". A screen 6: there is a large empty band between "Order completed by recency" and "Show sub-issues", and again before "List options", so the popover looks unfinished. A screen 1: the Revert/Reviews block and the chat panel float with no container, and there is a big gap between the PR card and the reply box.
