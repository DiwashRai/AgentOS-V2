# subissues: gaps

Reference: `10-docs-13-parent-and-sub-issues.png`, the whole image (2880x1422), scale 2.86 (cap height of 13px text measured at 27.2 ref px on "Display" D and "Labels" L), theme light. Linear's numbers below are in CSS px at that scale.

## Things the kit has no piece for

- **Popover (a panel of settings):** Linear opens a floating panel under the Display button, anchored to its end, holding pickers, a button and pills, on a #d6d6d6 hairline with a soft drop shadow, laid over the rows / the kit's only popover sheet (`Popover.module.css`) is internal to Menu, Select, Segmented and the account sheet, and each of those draws only a list of rows, so nothing can hold arbitrary content or float over the page / I used `Surface level="sheet"` (white, 1px ring at ink 9%, 10px radius, no shadow), placed as a column between the rows' leading cells and their faces. The rows that Linear's popover covers are left out, and the popover's edge is softer and has no lift.
- **Rule or divider:** Linear draws a full-width hairline under the Sub-issues head (81 to 927, under the head's centre by 17.5) and two edge-to-edge rules between the popover's sections (Ordering | Completed issues | Display properties) / the kit has no divider, and no Section variant with a ruled head. Only Checklist `look="page"`, Row `item`, Table's column head and foot, Tabs and Head `line` draw hairlines, and each comes with its own content / I left the rules out. The popover's sections are spaced apart instead (24 between the pickers, 32 before Display properties).
- **Fold caret in a section head:** Linear's head starts with a ▼ caret / `Section` has no slot before its title and no fold, and `Head`'s fold is a chevron in a list band / I composed the head from a quiet `Button size="sm" icon="caret"`, a Text and the progress, as the library's block head does. This also gave Linear's 13px/500 grey title instead of Section's 15px/600 ink-1 heading.
- **Skeleton/placeholder bar:** Linear's rows have 9px-tall, fully rounded #dcdcdc bars in place of titles (168, 95, 199, 54, 108, 138 and 100 wide) / nothing in the kit draws a placeholder / I used a blank `Chip tone="fill"` (no-break spaces as its label, sized to Linear's widths), which is 20px tall with a 6px radius at ink 9%. It reads twice as heavy as Linear's bar.
- **Placeholder face with a presence dot:** Linear shows 18px blank grey discs (#ebebeb) with a 6px green/grey/yellow status dot ringed in white at the lower right / Avatar always writes initials (an empty name becomes "?") and has no presence dot / I used `Avatar size="sm"` named with a zero-width space so the 16px neutral disc draws with no initials. The dots are left out (rows 1, 3, 4, 5 green, row 2 grey, rows 6 and 7 yellow).
- **Multi-select toggle pills that wrap:** Linear's display properties are one set of independent toggles (Status and Assignee on) wrapping onto 5 lines inside the popover / Segmented is single-select and never wraps (it overflows into a "+N" chip, or with `fit` it overflows the box) / I used one `Segmented fit` per visual line, each with its own pressed pill (or none). The look is right, but the semantics are not one group.
- **Glyphs:** Linear's Display button is a "sliders" glyph (three lines with knobs), its sort-direction button is an up arrow beside three shortening lines, and the Ordering label has an ↑↓ pair of arrows / the kit has no sliders, sort or order glyph / I used `compact` (four lines) for Display and `up` (the chevron) for sort direction. I dropped the ↑↓ glyph, because FormRow's label is a string (see below).
- **Status colours:** Linear's rows use a rose-brown Todo ring (#bd958e), a yellow half pie for In Progress (#e5bd00) and an orange quarter pie (#c9680f), each about 15px / Mark has only the kit's states, a grey `idle` ring (ink-3) and an orange `run` pie (#e8590c), at 14px / I used idle for the five Todos and run with progress 1/2 and 1/4 for the other two, so the yellow one is orange.

## Kit components that draw it differently, whatever the composition

- **Mark (progress after a title):** Linear's 0/7 progress is a 16px light-grey ring (about 2.5px) with a small blue dot at 12 o'clock / following PlanSection (`kind="run" small progress`), ours is a 10px orange 1.5px ring with an empty pie. It is smaller, and orange reads as "running".
- **Select (closed):** Linear's picker is a bordered input box (1px #dadada, white, about 6px radius, 26px tall) as wide as the column (Priority 123px, All 157px), its value at the left and its chevron at the far right / ours is a borderless chip, 28px tall, that hugs its value with the chevron right after it. Nothing sets a picker's width or gives it the field look. This is also why our popover is 297px wide where Linear's is 332px: its width comes from the pills, not the pickers.
- **Button (open state):** Linear's Display button is drawn in its open state, a #f1f1f1 filled 27px square with the glyph in near-black, while its popover is open / Button has no pressed or open state (only Menu and Select triggers do) / quiet button at rest, no fill.
- **Button sm square:** Linear's sort button is 26px. Ours is 28px (`--h-control-sm`) with the kit's shadow.
- **Segmented pill:** Linear's pill is 26px tall with about 6px radius. Pressed, it is white on a 1px #dfdfdf edge with near-black text, and unpressed it is grey text (#4c4c4c) / ours is 28px with an 8px radius, and pressed it is a grey fill (`--chip-on`, ink 9%) on a 14% edge with ink-1 text. The pressed pill reads as a grey slab where Linear's reads as a raised white key. Linear's line pitch is 30.5px and ours is 30px (28 + 2), which is fine.
- **FormRow label ink:** Linear's labels (Ordering, Completed issues, Display properties) are the same grey as the unpressed pills (about #4f4f4f) / FormRow's label is fixed at `--tk-label` (ink-3 #66666f), lighter than our pills' ink-2. I set "Display properties" to tone 3 to match the FormRow labels, so our popover has two greys where Linear's has one.
- **FormRow label:** it takes a string only, so the Ordering row can't have its leading ↑↓ glyph. The label column (128 + 12) does land the pickers exactly where Linear's start (140 past the label).
- **Row line height:** Linear's sub-issue rows are 40px apart (39.8) / Row `line` is 36px (`--h-line`), so our seven rows span 216px where Linear's span 239. The first row is placed level with Linear's first row, and the last one sits 20px higher.
- **Row line cell gap:** Row's column gap (10) and the `face` column (20) would put the bar 30px after the glyph's left edge, where Linear's is 23px. I put the glyph and the bar in one cell (a Stack with an 8px gap) to keep Linear's spacing.
- **Avatar sm:** 16px, where Linear's row face is 18px. The 18px `md` face is always in a person's hue with white initials, so it can't be a neutral placeholder.
- **Chip:** its height (20) and radius (6) are fixed, and inside a Row the default `line` tone turns into an outlined pill, so only `fill` gives a filled bar.
- **Theme ground:** Linear's page is #fcfcfc and its popover pure white. Paper's page is #ffffff, so our white sheet sits on white and only its 9% ring parts it from the page.

## Layout plumbing (no visual of its own)

- The section's inset from the image's edges (88px each side, 76px from the top) is made with two `Surface level="list"` pads (32 + 32) and Stack gaps around empty Stacks. The kit has no inset or width prop for a block on the page.
- The faces are kept on the rows' 36px pitch by a Stack of 20px items (the 16px disc plus a 4px gap to an empty Stack) 16px apart. This is because they are not in the rows' Table: the popover sits between them.
