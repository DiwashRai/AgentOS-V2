## Our screens against the current ones (the goal): your direction 1, the current screens 3, same 0

- **Judge 1** (A was your direction) picked the current screens. B wins narrowly. Screens 5 to 10 are pixel-identical. On the four list screens (My queue light and dark, Compact, Intake), the only difference is how groups are separated. A puts a roughly 12px empty strip, ruled at top and bottom, above every group header. It reads as a dead half-row or a rendering seam, not deliberate spacing, and it costs about 20px per group. B puts the tinted group header straight under the last row. That is tighter and cleaner, and the header fill alone separates the groups well enough. The space it saves pays off in Compact, where B shows the "Waiting on others" group above the fold and A pushes it off entirely.
  - your direction's gap: Remove the ruled empty strip above each group header in the grouped lists (My queue, Compact, Intake). Let the tinted header band sit directly under the previous group's last row so the groups separate without wasted rows, and so Compact shows as much as B does.
  - the current screens's flaw: With no gap left, a group's start depends on a low-contrast header tint, mostly in light mode. Intake's "+5 Show all 11" row runs straight into the "Platform backlog" header, and "Ready to start" in My queue looks like a stray row. Give group headers a stronger top rule or slightly more top padding inside the band, so a new group shows clearly without adding a gap.
  - Broken: Nothing truly broken in either. Screens 5 to 10 are identical in A and B. In B's Compact (screen 3), the first "Waiting on others" row is cut off under the footer bar. That is normal scroll clipping, but the half-visible row behind the footer looks sloppy and could use a fade or a solid footer edge. A's Compact cuts off below the Queued group.
- **Judge 2** (A was your direction) picked the current screens. The two are pixel-identical on screens 5 to 10 (ticket detail, runs tab, owner menu, file editor, Skills, agent editor). B runs the header flush after the last row. The tinted header band already separates the groups, so B's rhythm is tighter and more Linear-like, and in Compact it fits an extra group ("Waiting on others") above the fold that A pushes off-screen.
  - your direction's gap: Remove the empty spacer band above each group header in My queue, Compact and Intake, and let the tinted header bar sit directly under the previous group's last row, so a group break is one band, not a gap plus a band.
  - the current screens's flaw: With no gap, the switch from the "Your move" list to the "Agents at work" card grid on My queue is weak: the header band carries the whole transition, and the cards' bottom edge merges into "Ready to start". Give the card-grid section its own surface or a stronger top and bottom rule so a block that differs in kind still reads as a separate unit.
  - Broken: Nothing is broken in either. In both versions of Compact the bottom row is cut off behind the sticky footer, which is normal scroll overflow (in B that row is "Waiting on others"). In both versions of the owner menu the dropdown covers the stage track and the run line, which is expected for an open menu.
