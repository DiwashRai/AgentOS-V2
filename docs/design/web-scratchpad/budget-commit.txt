kit: the budget is the owner's checkpoint, and the card lists each raise

The owner's intent for the kit budget: a raise is fine when it's needed, but it
never happens unseen, and the pressure is there to make reuse the first choice.

- AGENTS.md: a change that needs more asks the owner (through the lead) with
  what it needs, the components it tried and why they don't fit, and the cost;
  the owner approves by adding the raises entry.
- kit.mjs: the card lists every raise against the base with its reason, under
  "Budget raised", where it used to print only the totals. The header comment
  says the gate is a checkpoint, not a wall.
- ledger.json: the components raise to 40 records the owner's own approval.
