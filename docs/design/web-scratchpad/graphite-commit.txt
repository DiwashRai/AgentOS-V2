web: Graphite, a flatter and more neutral kit look (design spike)

The starting point for the restyle gauntlet. Zinc greys with one indigo for
your move and focus; ink as the accent; rows and panels on hairlines with at
most a 1-2px shadow; rows light up instead of lifting; the status edge only for
your move, running and blocked; neutral mono project keys; a light rail ruled
off with a hairline. Only tokens.css and the Row, Button, Badge and Frame
modules change; no screen code. The kit is 26 lines smaller.

The screenshot baselines are not updated: this look is a proposal.
