What changed

Neutral greys, one accent

Zinc greys replace the warm, tinted neutrals. Indigo means "your move" and focus, and nothing else. Orange, red and green stay, but only on marks, ticks and meters.

ink muted rule you live blocked done

Flat on hairlines

Rows and panels sit on 1px hairlines with at most a 1–2px shadow. Rows no longer lift on hover; they light up. The grey well is now a faint tint, and the needs-you gradient is gone.

Quieter rows

The status edge is a short 2px tick, drawn only for your move, running and blocked. Done and queued rows have none. The open row gets a soft lifted ring instead of a black outline.

Neutral keys, crisper faces

Project keys are plain mono on grey, no longer cream or pastel. Faces keep one saturated hue each: violet, blue, pink, teal, slate.

Ink as the accent

Dispatch run and the logo use ink: black in light, white in dark. Buttons are 500 weight with a 6px radius, and Approve is flat indigo without its glow ring.

A light rail

The rail matches the page and is ruled off with a hairline, where it used to be a dark navy band in both themes.
