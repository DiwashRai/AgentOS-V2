gauntlet: separate by edge, not grey (the two style reviews)

An Opus and a Codex reviewer compared our screens with Linear's
(.review/gauntlet3/style). Their shared finding: Carbon drew controls,
tags, fields and open panels as +13 fills with no edge, where Linear
draws a +2..4 fill inside a hairline. Everything either raised is in;
on their three disagreements, Opus's call.

- Carbon: buttons, fields, search and picker chips a faint fill on a
  hairline; tags and keycaps their outline alone; hover still fills.
- Open rows and open runs: a card (the sheet's tone, a hairline), not a
  slab.
- The run card's Now foot and a popover's filter: a hairline, no band.
- Your own message: the card's tone on a hairline.
- The sheet has a rim: in Carbon a hairline, in Paper a line a step
  darker than the sidebar (Linear's Ash: #e3e4ec on #edeef3).
- Pressed filters: a control's face on a firm hairline, full ink, the
  accent in the count. Compose: a hairline circle.
- Paper: tag edges firm (white on the sheet was invisible); fields stay
  white under the pointer, their edge firming; bands lighter and less
  round; a lighter scrim; the off switch white on an edge.
- Library rows keep their kind's hue; ticket types lose the grey square;
  Intake's "+5" and failed query take an edge instead of a fill.
