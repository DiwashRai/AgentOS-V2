rows stayed in rounded patches with inset rules: the head and the row
each had their own extent.

  rows (a band, a hover's wash, the open card, the hairline between

  body, are already there). The joined-patch radius rules go.
- Chip: four tones that wash a hue into the tag with no edge, the glyph
  in the hue and the words in full ink, as Linear marks a project's
  resources: indigo, teal and amber (the kinds' hues) and neutral. How
  strong is the theme's (--chip-tint, measured on Linear's: about 12%
  in the dark). The project twin uses them.
