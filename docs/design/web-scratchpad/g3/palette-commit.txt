gauntlet: a cooler palette, faint tones, cards on hairlines

The owner's call: the light mode's grey made it drab, and in both modes
the grey slabs stood too far off the page (Linear's comment card is 3
levels over its sheet in the dark; ours was 6).

- Paper: tones, fills and lines mixed from a cool slate (--wash, --hair)
  instead of the ink, each step smaller; cool near-black and slate inks;
  a pale cool sidebar.
- Carbon: a touch cooler; card tones a third to a half of what they
  were (a card now 4 levels over the sheet); the sidebar 30% black, as
  near Linear's as its sheet.
- Card, the run card and the reply box take a hairline, so a card is
  told by its edge more than its shade.
- Group gap 10px everywhere.
