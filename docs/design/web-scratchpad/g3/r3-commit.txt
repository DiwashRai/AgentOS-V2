gauntlet: softer dark inks, a tinted open row, editors that fit

Round two's eight judges: dark titles read bright white and the
metadata sat nearly as bright; selected rows were parted by heavy
lines; the editors carried empty boxes and too many devices.

- Carbon's inks soften and step further apart (#e2e4ea, #a9adb9,
  #8b909d; the quiet one about 5.9:1 on the sheet); a property's head
  takes the quiet ink. A document's title 26px, clearly over its body.
- An open or selected row is one soft band of the accent (14% on
  Carbon, 7% on Paper), no edge, so rows open together read as one.
- An editor's text sheet is as tall as its text; History names the
  latest two versions, then "+N earlier".
- A stage card's phase is a quiet word, not a filled tag; its prompt
  line quiet text, not mono; a drag handle shows on hover or focus.
- All teams' state words in one plain ink; the glyph says the state.

A lighter medium on dark (450) was tried and dropped: the style lint
allows 400, 500 and 600 only.
