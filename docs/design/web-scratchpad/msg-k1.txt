kit: tooltips, menus, toasts, checkboxes and radios on Base UI

The owner raised the kit's budget to 45 components and 9,000 lines for these (2026-10-21).

- Tooltips: every title the kit takes is drawn as Linear's tooltip (Base UI's), one per frame and
  one per dialog. The words stay in the accessibility tree as a description, or not at all where
  they are the name already; a tip that repeats words on screen opens only while they are cut
  short. Esc closes a tooltip and still reaches the menu or dialog under it.
- Menu: a "..." button with a menu of actions; the ticket page and the peek use it to copy the
  ticket's link and key and to open its Jira issue and pull request.
- Toasts: useToast raises a short word that something was done (the copies); the frame and each
  dialog have their own region, so a toast raised in the peek shows over it.
- Checkbox and Radio: for the admin's intake settings (Settings, W8); the kit sheet shows them.

Tooltips and toasts take no slots (the frame draws them); Menu, Checkbox and Radio do: 43/45
components, 8,139 of 9,000 lines.
