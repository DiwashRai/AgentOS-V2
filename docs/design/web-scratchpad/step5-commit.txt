compose=…>` only to get a reply box, and Send was a span. The reply box is now
Composer: its own text, a real Send button (it sends through `onSend` when the
screens are wired; W0 is static), Ctrl/Cmd+Enter to send, pinned at a
full-height column's foot. Thread draws only the messages. The slot Menu freed
in step 2 pays for it: the kit is 40 of 40 components again, at 7,110 lines
against 7,695 before the consolidation.
